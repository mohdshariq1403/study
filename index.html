Setup Firebase Authentication
×

To enable cross-device sync, you need to setup Firebase Authentication:

  1. Go to Firebase Console
  2. Select your project "study-tracker-a207b"
  3. Click "Authentication" in the left sidebar
  4. Click "Get Started"
  5. Go to "Sign-in method" tab
  6. Click "Email/Password"
  7. Enable it and click "Save"

Without this setup, you can only use the app in Demo Mode.
